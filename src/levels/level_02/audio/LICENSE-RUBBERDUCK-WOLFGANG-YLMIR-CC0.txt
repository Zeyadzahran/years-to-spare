Level 2 ambience beds
=====================

city_rain.mp3    3.ogg from "Rain (loopable)" below, whole take, seamed to loop
city_day.mp3     ambient_01.ogg (a low machine hum) from the rubberduck pack
city_night.mp3   crickets.mp3 (Wolfgang_) with noise_01.ogg from the rubberduck pack, low in the mix, as the mains buzzing

From "Rain (loopable)" by Ylmir

(3.ogg - recorded at a window, 45 seconds)

From "30 CC0 SFX loops" by rubberduck
https://opengameart.org/content/30-cc0-sfx-loops
(ambient_01.ogg, noise_01.ogg)

From "Crickets Ambient Noise - loopable" by Wolfgang_, recorded by Ted Kerr

(crickets.mp3)

Attribution is not required, but the source is recorded here so the project's
audio provenance remains clear. The short loops were tiled to 30 seconds with
short crossfades and the layers mixed to one mono bed per time of day; every
file has its seam folded so it loops, then is loudness-levelled (see
LOUDNESS.txt) and encoded to MP3. The same rubberduck pack is the source of the
Level 1 boss voice.
