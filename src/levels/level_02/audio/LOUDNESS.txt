city_rain.mp3, city_day.mp3 and city_night.mp3 were normalized with ffmpeg loudnorm (two-pass, linear): the rain to -26 LUFS as the foreground layer, the day and night beds to -30 LUFS under it. Level 1's beds sit at -22 (wind) and -30 (bed). The rain is Ylmir's 45-second take as recorded, with its last second folded into its first so the file end flows into its start; the day and night beds are 30 seconds, tiled from their loops with half-second crossfades at every repeat and seamed the same way. All three are libmp3lame q2. The rain plays through the whole level; only the day and night beds crossfade at the gateway, on volume_db, in DayNightTransition.
