Level 2 music
=============

New_Factory.ogg
"new_factory_129bpm" from "T & T Free Cyberpunk Pack 2" by tricksntraps
https://opengameart.org/content/t-t-free-cyberpunk-pack-2

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Attribution is not required, but the source is recorded here so the project's
audio provenance remains clear. The original runs 130 s and fades to silence
over its last three seconds; it is cut on the bar line at 67 bars (124.65 s)
with a 50 ms fold so the end runs straight into the start, brought down 1.4 dB
so its peaks no longer clip, and re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis (q6). It loops at
the file's end via the level script, like Desert_Mystic.ogg. The pack's other
tracks were not used.
