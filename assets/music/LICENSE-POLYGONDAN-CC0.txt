Level 1 music
=============

Desert_Mystic.ogg
"Desert_mystic (dynastic Egyptian theme)" by PolygonDan, variation 2
https://opengameart.org/content/desertmystic-dynastic-egyptian-theme-cc0

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Attribution is not required, but the source is recorded here so the project's
audio provenance remains clear. Trimmed from 56s to 53s to drop the fade-out
tail so it loops, with a 0.4s fade-in and 0.8s fade-out at the seam, and
re-encoded from the original MP3 to Ogg Vorbis (q6). The level script loops it;
the loop point is the file's end.
