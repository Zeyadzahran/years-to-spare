Player voice
============

young-man-hurt.mp3   (hurt2.wav, compressed and soft-clipped up to the old grunt's level)
young-man-death.mp3  (death1.wav, as recorded)
boy-hurt.mp3         (hurt2.wav, pitch and formants raised 30%, same levelling)
boy-death.mp3        (death1.wav, pitch and formants raised 30%)
old-man-hurt.mp3     (hurt2.wav, pitch lowered 16%, slowed 12%, 6.5 Hz tremor, same levelling)
old-man-death.mp3    (death1.wav, same treatment)
From "Voice Clip Pack - Male Adventurer RPG" by Brandon Song (wolfwoot)
https://opengameart.org/content/voice-clip-pack-male-adventurer-rpg

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Attribution is not required, though the author asks for a credit; the source
is recorded here so the project's audio provenance remains clear. The adult
takes are loudness-levelled and encoded to MP3; the boy's are the same takes
shifted up with formants moved along (rubberband), so the bodies share one
actor and the younger one sounds smaller rather than sped up; the elder's are
shifted down, slowed and given a vocal tremor (ffmpeg tremolo) so the voice
reads as worn. The pack also
has attack, jump, victory and other takes in the same voice.
